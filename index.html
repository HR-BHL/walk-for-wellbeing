How to register

  1. Open the Walk for Wellbeing registration page.
  2. Select the London walk on Sunday 25 October.
  3. Join the team Bvlgari Hotel London.
  4. Complete the registration form with personal and emergency contact details.
  5. A confirmation email follows, with a link to a personal fundraising page.

Every walker must register individually. Registration is required for insurance purposes and cannot be completed on behalf of another colleague.

The deadline for joining the hotel team is Friday 9 October.

Fundraising

Each walker receives a personal fundraising page on registration. Every donation to a team member's page counts towards the Bvlgari Hotel London total.

Hospitality Action suggests a target of £80 per walker. There is no minimum, and taking part matters more than the amount raised.

All donations go directly to Hospitality Action. UK taxpayers can add Gift Aid, which increases every £1 donated by 25p at no extra cost.

Unable to attend on the day

Colleagues working on Sunday 25 October can take part through Walk It Your Way: any distance, in any location, on any day between Saturday 10 and Sunday 25 October.

These walks count towards the team total in exactly the same way. Registration follows the same steps, using the Walk It Your Way option.

What to bring

  • Comfortable, supportive walking shoes
  • Layers suitable for the weather
  • A refillable water bottle and snacks
  • A fully charged mobile phone
  • Emergency contact details
  • Any personal medication

About Hospitality Action

Hospitality Action is the UK charity supporting people who work, or have worked, in hospitality through times of hardship, ill health and difficulty.

Its services include an employee assistance programme, a confidential helpline, counselling and financial grants.
